Method

Same pipeline, every edition.

Four stages, in order, with the lead accountable at each gate. Nothing ships on one pass.

01 · research ×4

Parallel research passes

Spec, support matrix, ecosystem and security, trajectory — separate researchers, primary sources only, every claim carrying its URL and as-of date.

02 · synthesis

Lead synthesis

One writer turns the passes into a draft. Conflicting numbers stay side by side; announced and shipped stay distinct.

03 · citation pass

Independent verifier

Every load-bearing claim gets a numbered citation; every cited URL is fetched; unsupported claims are softened or cut, and the corrections are logged.

04 · adversarial review

Fresh-context reviewer

A skeptic re-fetches sources, re-measures headline numbers, and hunts for claim mismatches. Findings are severity-tagged and fixed before publication.

Full provenance, research notes, and verification logs live in the private research repository. The public repo carries the papers.
